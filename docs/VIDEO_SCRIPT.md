# Necro — demo video (under 3:00)

Judging: **Presentation** is 20% of the score and decides the **Best Demo** prize. The judges ask: does the video
show the project working end to end, and does it say what problem it solves, for whom, and why it matters?

## Rules before you record (official Kiroween rules)

- [ ] **Under 3:00.** Judges stop watching at 3 minutes. Aim for 2:45.
- [ ] Public on **YouTube**; paste the link on Devpost.
- [ ] Show the **real app working** (necro.malitos.tech), and only features that work on recording day.
- [ ] **No third-party trademarks:** don't name or show the old Google reader; **don't show the LinkedIn, X, Instagram
      or TikTok logos** (Connectors page, Resurrect tabs, previews), or blur them. Say "your social accounts".
- [ ] **No copyrighted music.** Use royalty-free or no music.
- [ ] English voice-over (or English subtitles).

## The pitch in three lines

- **Hook:** RSS was left for dead, but almost every site still publishes a feed.
- **Problem:** people who read to create drown in algorithmic feeds, tabs and copy-paste.
- **Solution:** Necro takes you from follow → read → understand → share, in one calm place, in your own voice.

### Recording tips

- Record at 1920×1080, browser zoom 110–125%, hide bookmarks and other tabs.
- Use a test account with 5–10 good feeds already summoned, so the Graveyard looks alive.
- Record each scene separately and cut; keep the voice-over steady and slightly fast.
- Add captions (YouTube auto-captions, then fix names like "Séance" and "Necro").
